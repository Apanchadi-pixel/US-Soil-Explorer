Soil texture triangle (USDA)

Points appear once sand, silt and clay are available for the selected depth interval.

Nationwide point lookups cover published U.S. soil surveys, including Alaska, Hawaii and U.S. territories where available. No map-unit response is not a soil property estimate. SSURGO provides representative map-unit/component horizon estimates, not measured properties at the precise GPS point. A map unit may contain multiple soil components. Depth-interval property averages (default 0–15 cm) require full per-property horizon coverage of the selected interval. Missing values remain blank. SOC stocks require OM, bulk density, and coarse-fragment information for every overlapping horizon. Exports retain pending/error points with blank soil properties. Estimated SOC (when exported) is OM/1.724 and is an approximation, not a laboratory result. The OSD link is an external soil-series reference description, not a site-specific pedon.
Source: USDA-NRCS Soil Data Access and Official Soil Series Descriptions. Runs entirely in your browser: just open index.html (Internet access required; no Python or server needed). Uploaded CSVs never leave your computer; only the queried coordinate pairs and SQL are sent directly from your browser to USDA. Map backgrounds: USGS National Map imagery/topographic tiles, optional community OSM tiles, or no basemap. USGS datasets and services are publicly available; check source attributions for individual imagery. No GIS license or R packages required.
